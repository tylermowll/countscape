# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Countscape welcomes people of every background and experience level. We are
committed to a collaborative environment where people can participate without
harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Respect privacy; do not pressure anyone to disclose identity, location,
  machine details, photos, event information, or private correspondence.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, evidence, and moderation.
- Help make discussions accessible to newcomers and people communicating in a
  second language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory language, or sexualized conduct.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, trolling, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information.
- Sharing private reports, photos, logs, config, or correspondence without clear
  permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
the project wiki, release channels, and other spaces where someone is
representing Countscape.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate GitHub-platform abuse, use GitHub's private report-abuse tools. For
a project-specific concern that needs maintainer action, open a minimal public
issue asking for a private conduct contact path; do not include names, evidence,
or sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, limit
participation, or ban participation depending on severity and pattern. They will
limit report details to people needed for review and will avoid conflicts of
interest where practical. Good-faith reports will not be treated as misconduct.

Questions about this policy may be raised in a public issue if they contain no
private incident details.
