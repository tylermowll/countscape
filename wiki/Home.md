## Editing policy

Published wiki editing is restricted to repository collaborators. To propose a
correction or recipe, open a
[public issue](https://github.com/tylermowll/countscape/issues/new/choose) using
synthetic data and link the affected wiki page. Do not post personal photos,
real messages or event details, private config, generated wallpapers, raw
diagnostics, monitor serials, home paths, or logs.

Repository documentation is authoritative whenever a wiki page and the source
repository disagree. Vulnerabilities must be reported privately under the
[security policy](https://github.com/tylermowll/countscape/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
